Routalk Pilot Privacy Policy

Effective: August 30, 2026

Routalk Pilot is a mobile-first development and deployment system. This policy describes information handled when Routalk Pilot connects to services such as Vercel.

Information we process

Pilot may process account and team identifiers, repository and project identifiers, deployment status, authorization information, and technical request data needed to perform user-requested development and deployment operations.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized users, provision and manage projects, observe deployments, verify production releases, maintain security, and operate Pilot's controlled workflow.

Credentials and authorization

Authorization credentials are used only to perform operations authorized by the user. Pilot is designed to avoid exposing provider secrets in user-visible packages, repository documentation, or logs.

Sharing

Information may be transmitted to infrastructure and development providers, including Vercel and GitHub, when required to carry out an operation requested by the user. Routalk Pilot does not sell personal information.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and verify the service. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect authorization information and operational data.

Your choices

You may revoke a connected provider authorization through that provider's account or integration settings. Revocation may prevent Pilot from performing operations that depend on that connection.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Routalk Pilot evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

Privacy or support questions may be directed through the contact information provided with the Routalk Pilot Vercel Integration.
